Scroll-Stopping AI Ads

We give your team a simple, proven checklist of exactly what quick videos and photos to capture on a smartphone right inside your clinic. After the patient has their consultation, our system automatically follows up with them. We make sure they had a great experience, help them with any financing questions, and gently push them to leave a 5-star review on your Google page so your clinic ranks higher.
